CHOOSING YOUR PROTEUS SNOWBOARD

Browse our growing selection of unique snowboard designs from community artists, riders, and in-house designers. You can choose from our small-batch “Off the Rack” snowboards that are stocked and ready to ship, or you can customize your own board from our “Made to Order” designs.
All Proteus snowboards feature our patented adjustable camber technology and are built to order in our own Colorado board shop.
